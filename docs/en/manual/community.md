# Community and templates {#社区与模板}

Save validated methods as resources for reuse with similar data. Team members can use them when granted the relevant permissions.

## Processing workflow templates {#数据处理流程模板}

Templates retain steps and parameters to reduce repeated configuration. Check data types, columns, units and import settings before reviewing whether the result suits the experiment.

Consistency does not replace a researcher's judgment about sample comparability or parameter suitability.

## Origin templates and themes {#origin-模板与主题}

Reuse figure layouts and styles. Before transfer, confirm groups, X/Y columns and templates. Results also depend on the local Origin environment and template compatibility.

## Research scripts and plugins {#科研脚本与插件}

Scripts reuse calculations; plugins extend imports, processing and other tools. Instrument and algorithm support depends on each resource and environment.

A community listing does not guarantee suitability for every research task. Check source, dependencies, version and parameters.

## Public and internal team resources {#公开与团队内部资源}

- Use public resources within their published scope.
- Internal templates and scripts are available only to authorized members.
- Signing in or joining a team does not grant access to everything.
- Method resources and laboratory research databases are separate systems; do not attach unauthorized experiment data to templates.

The website introduces resource types and usage. Actual browsing, retrieval and use take place in the software under permissions.
