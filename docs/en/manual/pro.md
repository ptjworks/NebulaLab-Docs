# Licensing and permissions {#授权与权限}

Feature licensing, member roles and resource permissions are separate. A license does not make someone an administrator, and administrators do not automatically read every private result.

## Feature licensing {#软件功能许可}

Released and development versions are transitioning between authorization systems. Check your installed status and corresponding release notes.

The older system distinguished Basic and Pro. Basic provided core processing and single-sample Origin export. Pro added batch and template exports, presets, workflow and plot templates, formulation tools, experiment databases and dataset export.

The newer direction uses laboratory accounts with authorized, unauthorized and relevant read-only states. Legacy activation instructions do not apply uniformly to every version.

## Members and devices {#实验室成员与设备}

After installation, sign in or request laboratory membership through the software. Laboratory rules determine approved members and devices. Availability also depends on version, licensing and server capabilities.

## Roles and data permissions {#角色与数据权限}

| Concept | Determines |
| --- | --- |
| Feature license | Use of a software feature |
| Member and device eligibility | Connection to laboratory services |
| Administrative role | Authorized governance of members, devices and dictionaries |
| Object permissions | Viewing, exporting, changing or publishing a specific resource |

Members may save local results and submit eligible data when licenses, sources and object permissions allow. Permission to view or export someone else's data does not necessarily permit republication.

## Uploading is not sharing {#上传不等于共享}

Sample sharing and result permissions are evaluated separately. Current cloud analysis results are creator-private; uploading, linking a readable sample or having an administrator role does not make them available to the whole group.

Internal workflows, Origin templates and scripts are shared with specifically authorized members.
